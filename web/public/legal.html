uploadmycut · last updated 2026-09-26

What this is

uploadmycut is an independent classroom project made by Dalton Fowler, a teacher, for his school district's classes. It is not a commercial product and has no accounts, ads or payments.

Safety

uploadmycut is not a safety system. It makes cut files for a CNC router with the teacher's bit, speeds and depths, and checks each file against the class limits (board edges, depth, speeds). The limits it applies reduce mistakes; they do not make a job safe.

  • A trained adult checks every file and supervises the machine for the whole job.
  • A CNC router spins a sharp bit at high speed. Eye and ear protection on, hands and hair away from the machine while it runs.
  • Pausing or stopping the machine does not stop the router: switch the router off too.
  • Clamp the board, keep clamps out of the cut, and run dust collection.

The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, as its open-source licence says.

Privacy

  • Your drawing never leaves this device. The cut file is made in your browser; nothing you draw is sent to the server.
  • The server keeps only the class setup (bit, materials, speeds, limits) for the teacher.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no ads, no tracking. Cloudflare adds a Web Analytics script to the site; this site's security settings block it, so it collects nothing.
  • The site runs on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare keeps short request logs (for example the page asked for, the time, the browser and the network address) for up to 7 days, to keep the site running. They are not used for anything else.
  • Your browser may keep your own settings and work (for example your theme, and your design and the material you picked) on this device only. Clearing site data removes them.
