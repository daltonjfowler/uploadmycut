How to cut

  1. Open a drawing (SVG) or pick a shape. Inkscape: File → Save As → Plain SVG. Canva: Share → Download → SVG. Google Drawings: File → Download → SVG. Text must be turned into paths first (Inkscape: Path → Object to Path).
  2. Place it on the board. Drag to move. Drag a corner to make it bigger or smaller. Drag the round knob to turn it. Stay inside the dashed line.
  3. Give each line a job. Click a line, then pick: Cut out goes around the outside and cuts it free, Cut hole cuts inside the line, Engrave follows the line, Pocket digs out the inside.
  4. Fix the red spots. Red means the bit is too thick to fit there. Make the drawing bigger, or give that line a different job.
  5. Preview, then download your .nc file. Give it to your teacher. Your teacher runs every cut.
