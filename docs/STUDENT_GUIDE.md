# uploadmycut: student guide

You make the cut file. Your teacher runs the machine. Nothing you do on this page can move the
machine.

## 1. Get a drawing onto the board

Three ways:

- **Open drawing**: an SVG file. SVG is a drawing made of lines, not a photo.
  - Inkscape: *File → Save As → Plain SVG*. Turn text into lines first: *Path → Object to Path*.
  - Canva: *Share → Download → SVG*.
  - Google Drawings: *File → Download → SVG*.
  - Tinkercad: export the top view as SVG.
- **Shapes**: a keychain tag, a coaster, a heart, a star and more.
- **Text**: type your name. *Block* letters are chunky. *Script* letters join up, so a name can be
  cut out in one piece. Make capital letters at least 15 mm tall.

The tan rectangle is your piece of wood. The dashed line is the edge to stay inside. The blue dot
is where the teacher zeros the machine (the front-left corner).

## 2. Place it

- Drag it to move it.
- Drag a corner square to make it bigger or smaller.
- Drag the round knob to turn it.
- The panel on the right has exact sizes, *Flip*, *90°*, *Copy*, *Delete*, and *Border*.
- Arrow keys nudge it 1 mm. Shift + arrow keys nudge it 10 mm.
- *Ctrl+Z* undoes, *Ctrl+Y* redoes.

## 3. Give every line a job

Click a line, then pick a job. Shift + click picks more lines.

| Job | What the bit does | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Cut out** (orange) | goes around the outside, all the way through | keychains, ornaments, puzzle pieces |
| **Cut hole** (purple) | goes around the inside, all the way through | the ring hole in a keychain, letter centres |
| **Engrave** (blue) | follows the line, not deep | names, drawings on a sign |
| **Pocket** (green) | digs out everything inside, to a depth | coasters, trays, raised letters |
| **Don't cut** (grey) | nothing | lines you do not want |

A name keychain: type your name, press **Border**. The border gets cut out and the letters become
a pocket. Want engraved letters instead? Click the letters and pick *Engrave*.

Cut-out parts keep small **tabs**, little bridges of wood that stop the part flying loose. Your
teacher cuts or sands them off after.

## 4. Fix what the bottom bar says

- **✕ red**: must be fixed before you can preview. For example, part of the cut is off the board.
- **⚠ orange**: allowed, but check it. **Red spots** on the board mean the bit is too thick to get
  in there, so that bit stays wood. Make the drawing bigger, or pick a different job.

## 5. Preview and download

Press **Preview cut**. You see the board as it will look after cutting, and about how long it
takes. Tick *Show the bit's path* to see where the bit goes. Type a file name and press
**Download**. Give the `.nc` file to your teacher.

Your design stays in this browser, so a reloaded tab brings it back. The trash button empties the
board (Undo brings it back).
